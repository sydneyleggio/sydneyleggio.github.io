Quantitative finance

Market Analysis

Random Matrix Theory Complete
S&P 500 Regime Detection via RMT
Filters the rolling S&P 500 correlation matrix using the Marchenko-Pastur law to separate noise eigenvalues from genuine market-mode structure. Hidden Markov Model over the cleaned correlation distance identifies volatility regimes. Includes out-of-sample minimum variance portfolio construction and Sharpe ratio comparison against a naive benchmark.

Network Analysis Complete
Sector Correlation Networks (MST)
Rolling minimum spanning trees of 25 sector ETFs and indices through the Feb-Apr 2020 crash, computed from 40-day Pearson correlation matrices. Tracks how network topology collapses during systemic stress and recovers as sector-specific signals re-emerge. Data and visualization live on the homepage.

Stochastic modelling

stochrisk Library

A Python library for fitting and comparing stochastic volatility models to financial time series data.

Phase 1 — Complete
Core Model Suite
GBM, Merton jump-diffusion, and truncated Levy flight implemented with MLE fitting, log-likelihood, BIC scoring, and Monte Carlo path simulation. Includes the interactive visualization on the homepage.
Phase 2 — In Progress
Heston & SABR Models
Heston stochastic volatility and SABR model implementation with characteristic function option pricing, calibration to implied vol surfaces, and smile analytics.
Phase 3 — Planned
Risk Metrics & Backtesting
Value-at-Risk and Conditional VaR estimation under each model, historical simulation backtesting with Kupiec and Christoffersen tests, and portfolio-level stress testing.

Matched Filter Design
GW Background SNR Optimization
Derives optimal cross-correlation and auto-correlation estimators for detecting the stochastic gravitational wave background in astrometric survey data. Computes Fisher information bounds on signal-to-noise ratio and characterises sensitivity as a function of survey depth and sky coverage. Connects directly to the first-author paper in preparation.
