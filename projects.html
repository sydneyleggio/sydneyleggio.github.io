Quantitative Finance · Random Matrix Theory Complete
S&P 500 Crash Risk Analysis
Filters the rolling S&P 500 correlation matrix using the Marchenko-Pastur law to separate noise eigenvalues from genuine market-mode structure. Hidden Markov Model classification over the cleaned correlation distance identifies volatility regimes. Minimum spanning trees of 25 sector ETFs and indices (Feb–Apr 2020 crash) track how network topology collapses under systemic stress and recovers as sector-specific signals re-emerge. Includes out-of-sample minimum variance portfolio construction and Sharpe ratio comparison against a naive benchmark.

Astrometric GW Detection · Signal Processing
Gravitational Wave SNR Simulation
Large-scale simulation pipeline for detecting gravitational waves via correlated angular deflections in stellar positions, adapting a pulsar timing array statistical framework to astrometry. Models signal-to-noise ratios across synthetic star catalogs to measure how detection sensitivity shifts with survey geometry and noise floor. Led a critical re-engineering of the pipeline to run at production scale while preserving exact numerical accuracy across all noise and signal regimes.
