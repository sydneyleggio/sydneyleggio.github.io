ABOUT ME

[Photo]

I am a physics M.S. student at the University of Southern California, graduating May 2027. My graduate research develops statistical estimators for detecting the stochastic gravitational wave background using stellar astrometry, work that sits at the intersection of signal processing, Bayesian inference, and large-scale survey data.

The skills I use every day in research, handling noisy high-dimensional data, building probabilistic models, and deriving optimal estimators, translate directly to quantitative finance and data science. I am actively building out that side of my work through projects in stochastic volatility modelling, random matrix theory, and network-based risk analysis.

My first-author paper, "Auto-Correlation versus Cross-Correlation Estimators for Gravitational-Wave Backgrounds in Astrometric Surveys," is in preparation for submission to Physical Review D.

I am open to roles in data science, quantitative research, and quantitative finance. Feel free to reach out via the contact page.
