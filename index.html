Auto-Correlation versus Cross-Correlation Estimators for Gravitational-Wave Backgrounds in Astrometric Surveys

S. C. Leggio et al. — to be submitted to Physical Review D

Develops and compares auto-correlation and cross-correlation estimators for extracting the stochastic gravitational wave background signal from astrometric survey data. Derives optimal statistical estimators and analyses sensitivity bounds for Gaia and future surveys.

Interactive visualization

Random Matrix Theory

The eigenvalue spectrum of a large random correlation matrix follows the Marchenko-Pastur distribution. Eigenvalues above the bulk edge carry genuine market information. Adjust the market correlation slider to see the market mode separate from noise.

Green curve: Marchenko-Pastur density. Orange bar: market-mode eigenvalue. Blue bars: noise bulk. Eigenvalues above the dashed line carry signal.

Network analysis

Market Correlation Networks

Minimum spanning tree of rolling 40-day pairwise correlations across 25 sector ETFs and indices, animated through the Feb-Apr 2020 crash. Edge color encodes correlation strength; node size encodes degree centrality.

During the crash, the network collapses: all sectors become highly correlated (green edges dominate, node degrees equalize). As markets recover, sector-specific structure re-emerges.
