First-Author Work

In Preparation · Physical Review D
Auto-Correlation versus Cross-Correlation Estimators for Gravitational-Wave Backgrounds in Astrometric Surveys

S. C. Leggio et al.

In preparation · to be submitted to Physical Review D

Gravitational waves leave a coherent quadrupolar imprint on the proper motions and parallaxes of distant sources. This work derives and compares auto-correlation and cross-correlation estimators for recovering the stochastic gravitational wave background (SGWB) from astrometric survey data. The analysis establishes optimal weighting schemes, computes Fisher information bounds on sensitivity, and characterises the dependence of signal-to-noise ratio on survey depth and sky coverage for Gaia and next-generation surveys.

Current Projects

The nanohertz stochastic gravitational wave background, recently indicated by pulsar timing arrays, encodes information about supermassive binary black holes across cosmic time.

Astrometric GW Detection
Correlated Proper Motion Anomalies from the SGWB
Gravitational waves leave a quadrupolar pattern in the proper motions of distant sources. Statistical estimators extract this signal from Gaia and future Roman and Theia catalogues via the Hellings-Downs-like correlation function for astrometric observables.
SGWB Characterization
Spectral Shape & Anisotropy of the GW Background
Constraining the spectral index and angular power spectrum of the SGWB using angular correlations in astrometric residuals, complementary to pulsar timing array measurements in a different frequency band.
SMBHB Population
Supermassive Binary Black Hole Demography
Linking the SGWB amplitude and spectral shape to the underlying population of inspiralling supermassive binaries across cosmic time via Bayesian hierarchical models connecting GW observations to galaxy evolution.
