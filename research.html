Publications

First-Author Work

In Preparation · Physical Review D
Auto-Correlation versus Cross-Correlation Estimators for Gravitational-Wave Backgrounds in Astrometric Surveys

S. C. Leggio et al.

In preparation · to be submitted to Physical Review D

Gravitational waves leave a coherent quadrupolar imprint on the proper motions and parallaxes of distant sources. This work derives and compares auto-correlation and cross-correlation estimators for recovering the stochastic gravitational wave background (SGWB) from astrometric survey data.

UVEX Science Team · 2025
Electromagnetic Follow-up to Gravitational Wave Events with UVEX

Coughlin et al. (incl. S. C. Leggio)

Published · arXiv:2501.14109

Strategies for electromagnetic follow-up of gravitational wave events using the UltraViolet EXplorer, including survey scheduling, kilonova detection prospects, and synergies with ground-based observatories.

UVEX Science Team · 2025
Optimal Follow-Up of Gravitational-Wave Events with UVEX

Coughlin et al. (incl. S. C. Leggio)

Published · arXiv:2502.17560

Optimal scheduling and follow-up strategies for gravitational wave events with UVEX, maximizing the probability of kilonova counterpart detection within mission constraints.

Notable Projects

Research and project work spanning gravitational wave detection, space mission science, and satellite engineering.

Kris Pardo Group · USC Physics
Astrometric GW Background Detection
Adapted a pulsar timing array statistical framework to the astrometric domain and re-engineered a large-scale SNR simulation pipeline for sensitivity analysis across synthetic star catalogs. Models correlated proper motion deflections from the stochastic GW background across varying survey geometries and noise models. Led critical re-engineering efforts to bring the pipeline to production scale while preserving exact numerical accuracy.
UVEX Science Team · U of Minnesota / Caltech
UVEX Kilonova Follow-Up Strategy
Co-authored two peer-reviewed papers on electromagnetic follow-up of gravitational wave events with the UltraViolet EXplorer (UVEX). Developed follow-up scheduling and optimization strategies for detecting kilonova counterparts. Contributed as undergraduate thesis work at the University of Minnesota; UVEX is a NASA mission led by Caltech. Investigated synergies with ULTRASAT.
SSRL · University of Minnesota
Project Manager — IMPRESS & EXACT
Project manager for two small satellite missions at the Small Satellite Research Laboratory: IMPRESS (NSF-funded CubeSat) and EXACT (AFRL-funded mission, transitioning from CubeSat to hosted payload). Managed project timelines, Gantt charts, and cross-team coordination through mission design reviews, budgets, and technical documentation.
